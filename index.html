Direct optimization of the probability of lesion origin in proton treatment planning for low-grade glioma patients

Joint research at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ) and Heidelberg Ion Therapy Center (HIT) on the direct integration of a voxel-based toxicity model into proton treatment plan optimization for low-grade glioma patients.
